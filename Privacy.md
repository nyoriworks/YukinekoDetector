# Privacy Policy for YukinekoDetector

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

YukinekoDetector is developed by NyoriWorks. This policy explains how the iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Safari Web Extension, and Share extensions handle information.

## Summary

YukinekoDetector does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. Image classification runs on your device using Core ML. We do not operate a server that receives images or detection results.

## Information processed on your device

To provide image analysis, YukinekoDetector may process:

- Images you select in the Share extension.
- An image URL or a web page URL you choose to share. The extension may retrieve the linked page or its preview image to find an image to analyze.
- Image and video preview frames visible on a web page when Safari Auto-Scan is enabled.
- Extension settings, such as whether Auto-Scan and result badges are enabled. These settings are stored locally by the browser.

This information is used only to display an on-device detection result. Images and results are not uploaded to NyoriWorks.

## Network activity

YukinekoDetector does not send images, detection scores, browsing history, or identifiers to NyoriWorks.

When you analyze a remote image or share a web link, the app or extension requests that image or page directly from its original host. That host may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and user-agent, as it would when you visit the page in a browser. The extension popup also loads font files from Google Fonts; Google may receive a standard request when those fonts are loaded.

## Data collection and tracking

YukinekoDetector does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, cookies, accounts, or cross-app tracking. It does not create a user profile.

## Data retention

Analysis input is kept in memory only for the current analysis. The Share extensions release the image and model after an analysis finishes or is dismissed. Local extension preferences remain on your device until you change them, remove the extension, or clear browser data.

## Children

YukinekoDetector does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this location with a revised date.
